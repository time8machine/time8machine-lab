# Physics of Knowledge

UII investigates intelligence as a process occurring through coupling among a system, its representations, actions, environment, and feedback.

Reality → Observation → Representation → Imagination → Experimentation → Action → Feedback → Evaluation → Reorganization → Reality.

The central empirical question is whether increasing the fidelity and usefulness of this loop enables a system to expand the space of transformations available to it.

## Core constructs

- Reality: states external to the agent that constrain consequences.
- Representation: structures used to model states, relations, or possibilities.
- Imagination: construction of counterfactual states or transformations.
- Experimentation: deliberate intervention designed to discriminate among possibilities.
- Feedback: information produced by consequences of interaction.
- Reorganization: persistent alteration of representations, policies, architecture, or procedures.
- Transformation space: the experimentally defined set of transformation classes accessible under specified conditions.

The framework deliberately separates philosophical interpretation from measurable operational definitions.
