# UII: Physics of Open-Ended Transformability

This directory establishes the first reproducible research program for the Undergraduate Intelligence Initiative (UII) within time8machine.

## Core principle

> Intelligence is the capacity of a physical system to generate, evaluate, and enact transformations of its own organization, representations, and environment while remaining capable of expanding or reorganizing the space of transformations available to it.

“Infinite transformation” is philosophical shorthand for potentially open-ended transformability, not literal omniscience, unlimited computation, or a completed actual infinity.

## Research objective

Determine whether intelligence can be operationally characterized by a system's capacity to generate transformations, evaluate them against feedback from reality, enact selected transformations, retain successful organizational changes, and expand or reorganize the space of transformations available to it.

## Program progression

1. Formalize OET and distinguish it from closed adaptability.
2. Build ROH-Bench synthetic worlds.
3. Establish measurable transformation-space proxies.
4. Test feedback fidelity and residual obstruction.
5. Test intrinsic exploration and imagination.
6. Test self-reorganization.
7. Test embodiment.
8. Test social development.
9. Test transfer across changing bodies.
10. Build LEARN as an autonomous experimental agent.
11. Introduce machine-generated hypotheses only within preregistered scientific constraints.
12. Replicate and independently audit findings.

## Scientific rule

AI may generate code, analyses, hypotheses, and candidate experiments. It must not unilaterally define evidence, alter preregistered primary outcomes, or declare a hypothesis confirmed.
