# UII Research Questions

## Primary question

Under what physical and informational conditions does intelligence emerge as an open-ended developmental process in which a system can generate, evaluate, enact, and recursively reorganize transformations that expand or reorganize its future space of representations, actions, goals, and adaptations?

## Core questions

1. Can open-ended transformability be operationally distinguished from closed adaptability?
2. What constitutes a genuinely new transformation class rather than a novel instance of an existing capability?
3. Can an agent transform the process by which it generates transformations?
4. How do feedback fidelity, latency, noise, and dimensionality affect transformation-space expansion?
5. Is embodiment necessary, sufficient, or merely facilitative?
6. What minimal developmental priors permit transformation-space expansion?
7. Does intrinsic motivation increase open-ended development relative to externally imposed optimization?
8. Does counterfactual imagination accelerate or deepen transformation-space expansion?
9. Does social interaction generate transformation classes unavailable to isolated agents?
10. How does transformation-space growth change over developmental time?
11. What happens when the transformation-generating mechanism itself becomes a target of transformation?
12. Which environmental, architectural, informational, or energetic constraints cause transformation-space collapse?
13. Can developmental intelligence transfer across changing morphologies?
14. Can machine-generated research proposals improve discovery without corrupting preregistered inference?
15. What measurements reliably distinguish intelligence-as-function from mere output novelty?

## Long-horizon question

How does a physical system become capable of changing the space of things it can become?
