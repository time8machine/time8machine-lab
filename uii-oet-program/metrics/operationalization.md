# Operationalization Plan

## Transformation-Class Expansion

TCE = number of post-interaction transformation classes minus number of pre-interaction classes.

## Novel-class persistence

Fraction of newly acquired classes reproduced after a delay or context shift.

## Transformation-generation rate

TGR = change in transformation classes divided by developmental time.

## Transfer

Whether a newly acquired strategy class remains effective in a structurally related but superficially different world.

## Feedback efficiency

Change in successful transformation acquisition as feedback quality is manipulated.

## Residual obstruction

A descriptive measure of persistent prediction/action errors remaining after adaptation.

## Measurement caution

No metric should be interpreted as a scalar intelligence score until construct validity, reliability, discriminant validity, and sensitivity to confounds have been demonstrated.
